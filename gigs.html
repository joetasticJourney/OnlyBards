Gig #9: Caer Dineval Keep (Kedroth's private concert / mass-drugging cover) Caer Dineval · Session 6: O'Cosby's Revenge A full-band performance in the Caer's grand hall for acting speaker Kedroth and his dozen Knights of the Black Sword — explicitly staged as a cover for Roadie's Cosby-wine mass-drugging. Scales glimpsed the platinum dragonborn mid-set; Kaldur's magecrafted bass line pushed the DC-18 performance meter over the top; by song's end every Knight was face-planted and Kedroth was grappled in chains. Payment was formally a 'tip bucket' nothing-burger during, with Cranach paying 200 gp per band member after the keep was retaken. One of the band's most 'successful' gigs by body count: Kedroth killed, Avarice killed, the Caer liberated. ~1200 gp from Kedroth's chest + 200 gp per band member from Speaker Cranach afterward

Gig #7: Denev's Rest (impromptu house-party exorcism) Caer Dineval · Session 5: Makes House Party look like House Party 2 Roadie's self-booked 'house-party-as-exorcism' blowout. Two kegs on top of the Chuggy Pig, 240 beers at 1 sp each sold to the entire adult population of Caer Dineval, parade through town, dump the crowd into the abandoned Denev's Rest while the band fights the Sunblight Duergar upstairs. The crowd peaked somewhere around Woodstock '99: a militia man beaten to death, a Mind Master pummeled apart with bare hands, an eleven-year-old boy devouring a Duergar, a middle-aged woman topless screaming 'anarchy.' Scales snorted a bar line and took his pants off. Dreadie Color Sprayed to escape. Roadie saw Dreadwurst in the crowd and nearly relapsed before Dreadie's bone-pick test called him back. The ghosts were in fact Duergar — close enough. Rourke paid out anyway. 'This makes House Party look like House Party 2.' 24 gp (240 beers at 1 sp each) + 120 gp (20 gp per band member from Rourke afterward)
successful

Gig #5: East Haven prison yard East Haven · Session 4: We Didn't Start the Fire Double burning — Dzan the Red Wizard and William O. Cosby. 200-person crowd, biggest show the band has played. Enjee locked a four-on-the-floor pocket, Natty straddled a cannon mid-set in Cher-on-the-Battleship pose with Scales' Faerie Fire glowing her orc backside pink/violet, Dreadie debuted 'Fat Bottom Orcs,' Rico cast a Dancing-Lights crescent moon over the pyre, and Cosby's +13 charisma made both prisoners' death-screams actually harmonize. Mid-set Roadie flashed back to Lich-Brisket days, took a cursed pick from an undead Adidas-hooligan, transformed into a Dreadwurst-flavored shredder for a two-minute solo nobody could locate, then dropped the pick and reverted. Band crit the show; Havel reports gigs now pouring in across the Ten Towns. roughly 100 gp (base 30 + 20 scream-harmony + Argiloth's doubled fee for adding Cosby to the pyre)
successful
Gig #4: Wet Trout East Haven · Session 3: By the Power of William Moon Rico's Chaos Wolf debut. Band name announced as 'Chaos Wolf,' spelled Q-H-A-O-S V-U-L-F. cheap noon-slot pay, exact amount stiffed on entry — 'guy said he'd give you $10 but you're only going to get $8'
