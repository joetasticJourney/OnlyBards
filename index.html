Open quests 19

Recover Uror's Shears before the Cult of Ragnarok The Norn shears — Uror's Shears, said to cut anything and then return to the Norns after a single use — were stolen from Speaker Daneth's office by the one-armed Fang Finn Oaf, who cached them in the William Moon barrow. The Cult of Ragnarok / Church of Inexorable Ruin, under Thrynne the Black, wants them to shear the mythic Chaos Wolf awake so it devours the sun. The party has caught up with Finn Oaf inside the barrow and are jointly fighting off cult reinforcements. Roadie's proposed endgame: use the shears once — on a rope, on nothing important — so they vanish back to the Norns and the cult never gets them. from Prudence / Speaker Daneth
Get a bag of holding big enough for Enjee's drum kit The White Lady pointed Enjee to two shops for a drum-kit-sized bag of holding: Triglio in Targos and Ewan's Trinkets in Bremen. from White Lady seance
Investigate Kerr Koenig the corpse — a place where spirits yearn to rock The Kaer Konig corpse is the Shardalyn-corrupted dragon on Moonsong Hill. The band needs to purify it with Virgats the Wretched's help, then play a show on top of it as a stage. Virgats is onsite as of the end of this session, chanting over the dragon with his cudgel planted in the ground. The band still owes the show. from Thrynne the Black (via note at High Plume Station)
Meet Thrynne the Black's contact at High Plume Station The interrogated cultist confessed under torture that the shears were to be delivered to a contact in a white tunic at High Plume Station — one of the new geothermal way-stations that have popped up over the past year as rifts opened in the ice. from Interrogated cultist
Investigate Argent Hall (Enjee's cylindrical stone) A cloaked courier slid a stone cylinder to Enjee at the Big Fat Knucklehead; the coordinates triangulate to Argent Hall, an old moon-stronghold in the Spine of the World taken from the Duergar generations back, with a Selenite temple on-site. Kaldur recognized it immediately as tied to his king and his king's old friends. Who wanted Enjee sent there, and why, is unknown. from Cloaked courier at the Big Fat Knucklehead
Hunt down the Cold-handed Man (Sifos Cifik) A man rumored around East Haven as the 'Cold-handed Man' — real name Sifos Cifik — has been snatching people and sacrificing them to a cold winter entity in the old Aurel style. Attempted to drag Samantha out into the killing cold from the Wet Trout mid-gig; Kaldur and Roadie broke through a window and rescued her but he escaped. He leaves a cool-hand-shaped scar of dead flesh on his victims. from Samantha at the Wet Trout
Expose and dismantle the Children of Splintered Ice The band wiped the Children-of-Splintered-Ice tower cell on the northwestern shore (Felith and two cultist-ambushers dead, one tower-top jumper intimidated to death by Scales) and uncovered a signed hit list implicating 'Ice Knife' as the East Haven cell master. 'Ice Knife' is Nemitra, the silver-dragonborn owner of the Wet Trout, who was arrested on the spot by Captain Argiloth. The cult serves Cryonax (not Aurel). Scope beyond East Haven — and the Cold-handed Man's exact relationship to the cell — remains open. from Investigation at the Children-of-Splintered-Ice tower
Recover Dzan's simulacrum and crack his lair Before Dzan was burned, he gave up the location of his lair — under the Lucky Liar inn in Lonelywood. His simulacrum is somewhere in the frozen tundra, guarded by a wight; recovering the simulacrum is the only way into the lair. Lonelywood is on the route past Termalane, so the party plans to pick up Kaldur's Dumathoin gem-mine lead (Termalane) and the Lucky Liar lair on the same loop. from Dzan the Red Wizard
Deal with Dreadwurst and Lich Brisket Roadie's old band — Lich Brisket, a Limp-Bizkit-styled band fronted by a lich called Dreadwurst — went full necromancy at Bloodstock '99 and burned the 'non-nookie compliant' crowd alive. Roadie quit. Dreadwurst didn't take it well. Last session's hallucinated Adidas-hooligans turn out to have been real undead Lich-Brisket agents; one of them handed Roadie a cursed guitar pick mid-gig and briefly took him over. Roadie plans to use Uror's Shears to behead Dreadwurst. from Roadie, post-gig confession
Investigate Dugan's Hell Dugan's Hole, roughly south of East Haven, has been overrun by an unknown cult. Signs on the road now warn travelers off, with locals crossing out 'Hole' and writing 'Hell.' Rico has publicly committed the band to 'putting the Hole back in Hell.' from Travel rumors, Ten Towns map review
Travel to Skaldholm for Fenrir lore Dzan named Skaldholm — an island west of the Purple Rocks — as the single richest source of Skald lore about Fenrir and the chains of Gleipnir. Very far from Icewind Dale, almost certainly a late-campaign destination, but now on the map. from Dzan the Red Wizard
Hunt down the last Sunblight Duergar The 'ghosts' at Denev's Rest were in fact the last remnants of the Sunblight clan — Deep-Duergar that Kaldur's uncle had thought wiped out generations back. The band killed most of them in Denev's Rest but one Soul Blade turned invisible and jumped out a window at the end of the fight. Kaldur plans to track him through the clan-engraved gold the Duergar left behind — a diviner may be able to use it as a focus.
Expose and dismantle the Cryonax-Levistus alliance Cranach warned the band of a one-legged dwarven seer in his household who had foretold Cryonax being risen in alliance with Levistus. The Caer was the local foothold — the Shardalyn-sword Knights of the Black Sword, the Levistus altar in the basement, Avarice's Levistus wizardry — but it's a cell of something much bigger. Both lords of cold have aligned. from Speaker Cranach
Dispose of or contain the Levistus ice shrine in the Caer basement In the basement of Caer Dineval, an eight-foot ice pillar containing a horned Levistus-figure wrapped in black chains sits as a working altar. Touching the ice gives instant frostbite; fire has no effect on it. The DM has flagged the ice itself and the chains as cursed. The party left it in place for now rather than disturb it; Roadie is still being tempted by it as a potential stage prop. from self-assigned
Return Hwar-war to his mother in Bremen Hwar-war, the young Caer Dineval gatehouse guard the band Charm-Personed into surrendering, is from Bremen. His mother is a tavern-wench at a tavern called Hidden Treasures. His cover story to his mother is that he's dead; the band intends to deliver him alive next time they're in Bremen. from Hwar-war
Travel to Moonsong Hill to learn Rico's true lineage Oracle Ethel Arkoron told Rico to seek Moonsong Hill to the south if he wishes to know the truth of his lineage. Rico believes this is where his father was conceived and will unlock the next level of blessing from William Moon. Dreadie and Enjee privately noted that Moonsong Hill is actually a major religious site for the Selünite people (not the William Moon people). William Moon personally admitted this session that he does not know where Moonsong Hill is. from Ethel Arkoron
Recover a Shining Falls Delzunian crypt for Kaldur Oracle Ethel Arkoron told Kaldur of a secondarily-Delzunian crypt behind the Shining Falls, west of the Gray Peak Mountains, east of the Direwood, south of Loudwater. The crypt belongs to an old explorer type who may have Delzunian artifacts in his tomb. from Ethel Arkoron
Destroy Bonothin Decavis's phylactery in the Abyss Bonothin Decavis's phylactery is in the Blood Swamp of Grotzt — the third layer of the Abyss, a former Orcus realm now contested and held by Graz'zt. Characterized by viper trees and poisoned air. Roadie now has a confirmed destination for using Uror's Shears on the phylactery binding, which is the only way to make a Decavis beheading stick. Vrock the undead bat has confirmed to Roadie that this is the only way to undo what Roadie agreed to with Lich Brisket. from Ethel Arkoron / Vrock
Unseat Trovis as speaker of Kaer Konig Pinia Galvain, a halfling citizen and longtime Chaos Wolf fan, is running against the drunk sociopathic incumbent Trovis in two weeks. The band has implicitly endorsed her campaign by letting her clonk Trovis unconscious at the Northern Light. Trovis is currently in the paddy wagon. from Pinia Galvain
