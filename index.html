Recover Uror's Shears before the Cult of Ragnarok The Norn shears — Uror's Shears, said to cut anything and then return to the Norns after a single use — were stolen from Speaker Danneth's office by the one-armed Fang Finulf One-Hand, who cached them in the William Moon barrow. The Cult of Ragnarok / Church of Inexorable Ruin, under Thrynne the Black, wants them to shear the mythic Chaos Wolf awake so it devours the sun. The party has caught up with Finulf One-Hand inside the barrow and are jointly fighting off cult reinforcements. Roadie's proposed endgame: use the shears once — on a rope, on nothing important — so they vanish back to the Norns and the cult never gets them. from Prudence / Speaker Danneth
Find Bot Withers Recover or locate Bot Withers, a missing strongheart halfling regularly seen at the East Haven docks (Big Fat Knucklehead inn). The White Lady says he is on the 'eastern shore, north.' A fisherman at the Big Fat Knucklehead confirmed Bot launched from the dock on his fishing keel-boat over seven days ago, sober, and 'there's no way he's still alive.' The party is now on the East Haven ferry heading up the northeastern shore to look. from White Lady seance
Play the public burning of Dzan the Red Wizard Speaker Danneth booked the band to play the public execution of Dzan — a Red Wizard agent caught trying to 'awaken Zlan.' 30 gp base, plus 20 gp extra if the band can harmonize their music to the prisoner's screams. Day after tomorrow. from Speaker Danneth
Get a bag of holding big enough for Enjee's drum kit The White Lady pointed Enjee to two shops for a drum-kit-sized bag of holding: Triglio in Targos and Ewan's Trinkets in Bremen. from White Lady seance
Investigate Kerr Koenig the corpse — a place where spirits yearn to rock The White Lady's answer to Dreadie's question about places in the land where 'the spirits yearn to rock' was: 'Kerr Koenig, the corpse.' No further context yet. from White Lady seance
Meet Thrynne the Black's contact at High Plume Station The interrogated cultist confessed under torture that the shears were to be delivered to a contact in a white tunic at High Plume Station — one of the new geothermal way-stations that have popped up over the past year as rifts opened in the ice. from Interrogated cultist
Investigate Argent Hall (Enjee's cylindrical stone) A cloaked courier slid a stone cylinder to Enjee at the Big Fat Knucklehead; the coordinates triangulate to Argent Hall, an old moon-stronghold in the Spine of the World taken from the Duergar generations back, with a Selenite temple on-site. Kaldur recognized it immediately as tied to his king and his king's old friends. Who wanted Enjee sent there, and why, is unknown. from Cloaked courier at the Big Fat Knucklehead
Hunt down the Cold-handed Man (Sifos Cifik) A man rumored around East Haven as the 'Cold-handed Man' — real name Sifos Cifik — has been snatching people and sacrificing them to a cold winter entity in the old Aurel style. Attempted to drag Samantha out into the killing cold from the Wet Trout mid-gig; Kaldur and Roadie broke through a window and rescued her but he escaped. He leaves a cool-hand-shaped scar of dead flesh on his victims. from Samantha at the Wet Trout
Investigate the shrunken-Duergar hole in the East Haven ferry Roadie and Kaldur inspected the East Haven ferry (operated by Scython Mark 10) en route north and found what looks like normal wear but is in fact a deliberately disguised entry hole in the hull, sized for a Mind-Master Duergar to shrink through. Kaldur chopped it open on the way out promising to mend it after 'the rat hunt.' from Investigation aboard the East Haven ferry
