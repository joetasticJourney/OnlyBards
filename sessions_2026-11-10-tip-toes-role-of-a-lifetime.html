Session 9: Tip Toes: Role of a Lifetime Read transcript →

The Moonmobile rolls out of Caer Konig toward the Dwarven Valley, with the Chuggy Pig taking the longer road. Roadie has a new addition in tow — Alisander, the ex-Caer kennel boy, now dressed, styled, and accessorized as a miniature Roadie with his own tiny fan. Rico is already workshopping jokes for dwarves; Kaldur is bracing for the humiliation of pulling up to his ancestral home in the band's Chaos Wolf van.

At the gate, Vatsnil and Jorsite grill the party; Kaldur vouches, the band is let in. In the shrine to Klangadin Silverbeard, Rico rolls a 21 on religion and immediately uses the lore to call the whole room 'a bunch of fucking infidels' out loud. Kaldur lays his old battleaxe on the altar as an offering; Klangadin — through his proxy Naval Gravelgut, Kaldur's great-great-great-great grandfather — blesses it to +1. Rico tries to save himself with a 'your pickaxes look like moons, Saloon's blessing on you' crack that only lands after Dreadie rescues the room with 'what do you call a piano down a mineshaft? A flat minor.'

In Paxton's forge the band hits the mother lode. Paxton — Kaldur's old mentor, back from a rough Feywild stint — is working on Mark 8 of his iron-man suit while Mark 7 does the surgical wiring. Scales gets examined like a specimen: scale shaving, gem shaving, oil sample. Paxton's verdict is that Scales is a brass–topaz chromatic × gem hybrid, 'never seen before,' carrying two sources of divine blood. Paxton forges a brass bell onto Scales' saxophone that doubles his breath-cone, hands him a spirit-catching glove triggered by a Backstreet-Boys 'bye bye bye' gesture, and Dreadie picks up a dwarven rapier. Kaldur, out of pride, refuses to ask for plate.

In the mess hall Rico loses a drinking contest in two sips of gut-buster brew, snores off, and later projectile-vomits on Dreadie face-to-crotch while being carried in. Enjee draws tick-marks all over Rico's unconscious face with forge ash. Still drunk, Rico drags bar wench Mammy Reglands of the Longbags clan into the bathroom; mid-coitus he casts Silent Image to disguise her as Scales so he can finish 'for the moon.' Enjee walks in, assumes Rico is banging Scales, and immortalizes the vision with Minor Illusion as a painting. 'Gay dragon porn at breakfast,' complains a passing miner.

Lord Stokely Silverstream quotes 100 gp per dwarven member and 20 gp per non-dwarf. Roadie commissions a DC-25 latex disguise to turn Rico into 'Mooney Moonbeard of the Nesme Moonbeards' and pocket the difference; Kaldur openly washes his hands of it. Scales, hungover, botches his opening solo by six, burns heroic inspiration to recover, and then lands a 23-against-22 perfect harmony with Rico that reduces the crowd to tears. Stokely shakes Mooney's hand on the floor, ups the non-dwarf rate to 30, and walks away with a copy of the Book of Munnen Rico slipped him. In the bathroom, Dreadie survives 'the most incredible titty-job of his life,' briefly considers marrying Mammy Reglands, and makes his CON save.

Rolling out of the valley, Kaldur punches Rico in the mouth for the stunt. William Moon jogs on his back alongside the Moonmobile, kiss-finger-heals the lip, and whispers that Rico is his 'special play' who has 'never done anything wrong.' At the Blue Clam in Tourmaline the band meets Gulbrek the Wide — a full-blooded ogre with a scholar's intellect and a three-dragon-ante habit — who operates through the touched-in-the-head Vernon Bragg as a tax shield and his accountant Tickacticket (nicknamed Tick, or Connecticut) as signatory. Speaker Oris Mastiu books the band for tomorrow; Rico immediately insults him by theorizing aloud that half-dwarves and half-orcs are 'two inferior species put together.'

At the gem-mine mouth the blood reads clearly: one human source, a lot of gnome blood, rubbery brain tidbits — intellect-devourer residue. Rico declares a new tenet on the spot: psionics are unclean. Inside, the recurring 'three kobolds in a trench coat' from Kaldur's Greedy Six days ambush them. Kaldur Hail-Marys his axe and cleaves Thwip down to the lower jaw; Scales pinches the wound shut and pushes Cure Wounds through it, resurrecting him. Rico re-christens him 'Shablagoon' and grants him a crescent-moon scar; William Moon manifests water wings and a crescent-moon helmet for Thwip, plus a crescent-moon diaper bag for Dreadie. Deeper in, Scales walks up to the uncannily eloquent 'Trex the Glorious,' smacks him with the glove, and slurps the Black Ghost of the Greedy Six out of his eyes and nose, attuning the Glove of the Grim Fandango.

The freed kobolds — now worshipping Scales as a dragon — try to mine for him by throwing rocks at the walls. Kaldur signs for a six-dwarf claim via Tick. Deeper still the band finds the lost dwarven mining party: gnomic ceremorphs, flanked by one unusually muscular 'mesomorph' mind flayer. Roadie suggests simply asking the kobolds for drugs; Scales delivers; Rico face-chugs the entire bag of crushed red crystals as initiative is rolled.
