World 8

The Chaos Wolf (Vanagander) A Ragnarok-scale beast the cult intends to wake by shearing it with Uror's Shears. Prophesied to 'devour the sun.' The Fangs of Vanagander are named after it — sworn to prevent that awakening. Also spelled 'Chaos with a Q' in the party's proposed band-name workshopping. 2026-09-22-shearing-the-wolf
William Moon The moon-god of Rico Lunae's cult, apparently the local Odin analogue. Slain by three demons who cut up his body to build the world; his barrow preserves the story on a giant-slayer mural. Rico has convinced the party (with DM-rewarded gaslighting) that Odin was William Moon in disguise and Asgard is a realm inside the plane of Isgard alongside Argenteel, Selûne's domain. 2026-09-22-shearing-the-wolf
The Norns and Ginnungagap Norse-styled cosmology intersecting with Rico Lunae's Selûnian moon-god (William Moon = Odin). Ginnungagap is the yawning void of space between crystal spheres — a rigidly-gravitied plane in the William Moon barrow's entrance chamber. The three Norns are Urðr (age, looking back, wears white), Verðandi (present, wears earth tones and metal colors), and Skuld (hidden future, wears blues). Their shears cut fate itself and return to them after each use. 2026-09-22-shearing-the-wolf
Aurel A cold entity who used to receive sacrifices in the old ways — including through a ritual pattern the Cold-handed Man appears to be reviving. Aurel is dead: per Big Fat Knucklehead hearsay, killed years back by 'a gnome with a giant cock,' incinerated at the Black Cabin, and — per Kaldur's uncle's version, in which Kaldur's uncle saved him — reborn in Phoenix fire. Also lends his name to Aurel's Tears, a deep-blue drop of liquor served at the Wet Trout.

Location 7

Ten Towns of Icewind Dale The party is operating in the Ten Towns of the frozen north: East Haven (current base), Bryn Shander, Targos, Bremen, and others. Travel is by axe-beak mount or dog sled — or, for the truly reckless, by rocket-powered geyser sled with a decanter of endless water strapped to the back. Over the past year, geothermal rifts have opened across the ice, and new hot-spring 'way-stations' (like High Plume Station) have sprung up around them. 2026-09-22-shearing-the-wolf
White Lady Inn Seance-hippie bar and performance venue upshare in East Haven. Silks and multicolored lamps hang from the rafters; incense smoke perfumes the air. Rinaldo the halfling runs the seance service in the green room. Vardaban runs the bar. Named after the White Lady, Queen of the Lake. 2026-09-22-shearing-the-wolf
William Moon Barrow A stone barrow tomb about eight miles east of East Haven, marked with moon iconography. Contains: (1) an entrance chamber inscribed 'before ice, before flame, there was Ginnungagap — the yawning void' — a zero-gravity void trap; (2) an Odin's-eye stone tree yielding a 300 gp black-opal dagger when the left eye is pried out; (3) a well marked with the Erd rune where the eye must be thrown to reveal a stone staircase; (4) a giant-slayer mural of William Moon with a skald-riddle on the shield ('rattle-frame crafted into stone teeth tall') that opens a hidden door and collapses a titan statue; (5) a Norn's-loom room with three statues (Urðr, Verðandi, Skuld) and live spinning stone spindles trailing colored threads (golden, red, iron, gray, white, blue, earth) to be dressed onto the correct Norn; (6) a Gleipnir-and-Fenris-mouth trap that requires a Tyr-like sacrifice — putting your hand in the wolf's mouth deals psychic damage but does not actually take the hand; (7) a sarcophagus at the center, trapped to collapse the entire barrow if opened. 2026-09-22-shearing-the-wolf
Argent Hall An old moon-stronghold in the Spine of the World, wrested from the Duergar generations back by Kaldur's king and his king's old friends. Hosts a Selenite temple. Its exact coordinates arrived this session on a stone cylinder handed to Enjee by an anonymous cloaked courier at the Big Fat Knucklehead.
