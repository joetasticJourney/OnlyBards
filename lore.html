The world & its history

World 3

The Chaos Wolf (Vanagander) A Ragnarok-scale beast the cult intends to wake by shearing it with Uror's Shears. Prophesied to 'devour the sun.' The Fangs of Vanagander are named after it — sworn to prevent that awakening. Also spelled 'Chaos with a Q' in the party's proposed band-name workshopping. 2026-09-22-shearing-the-wolf
William Moon The moon-god of Rico Lunae's cult, apparently the local Odin analogue. Slain by three demons who cut up his body to build the world; his barrow preserves the story on a giant-slayer mural. Rico has convinced the party (with DM-rewarded gaslighting) that Odin was William Moon in disguise and Asgard is a realm inside the plane of Isgard alongside Argenteel, Selûne's domain. 2026-09-22-shearing-the-wolf
The Norns and Ginnungagap Norse-styled cosmology intersecting with Rico Lunae's Selûnian moon-god (William Moon = Odin). Ginnungagap is the yawning void of space between crystal spheres — a rigidly-gravitied plane in the William Moon barrow's entrance chamber. The three Norns are Urðr (age, looking back, wears white), Verðandi (present, wears earth tones and metal colors), and Skuld (hidden future, wears blues). Their shears cut fate itself and return to them after each use. 2026-09-22-shearing-the-wolf

Location 3

Ten Towns of Icewind Dale The party is operating in the Ten Towns of the frozen north: East Haven (current base), Bryn Shander, Targos, Bremen, and others. Travel is by axe-beak mount or dog sled — or, for the truly reckless, by rocket-powered geyser sled with a decanter of endless water strapped to the back. Over the past year, geothermal rifts have opened across the ice, and new hot-spring 'way-stations' (like High Plume Station) have sprung up around them. 2026-09-22-shearing-the-wolf
White Lady Inn Seance-hippie bar and performance venue upshare in East Haven. Silks and multicolored lamps hang from the rafters; incense smoke perfumes the air. Ronaldo the halfling runs the seance service in the green room. Vardaban runs the bar. Named after the White Lady, Queen of the Lake. 2026-09-22-shearing-the-wolf
William Moon Barrow A stone barrow tomb about eight miles east of East Haven, marked with moon iconography. Contains: (1) an entrance chamber inscribed 'before ice, before flame, there was Ginnungagap — the yawning void' — a zero-gravity void trap; (2) an Odin's-eye stone tree yielding a 300 gp black-opal dagger when the left eye is pried out; (3) a well marked with the Erd rune where the eye must be thrown to reveal a stone staircase; (4) a giant-slayer mural of William Moon with a skald-riddle on the shield ('rattle-frame crafted into stone teeth tall') that opens a hidden door and collapses a titan statue; (5) a Norn's-loom room with three statues (Urðr, Verðandi, Skuld) and live spinning stone spindles trailing colored threads (golden, red, iron, gray, white, blue, earth) to be dressed onto the correct Norn; (6) a Gleipnir-and-Fenris-mouth trap that requires a Tyr-like sacrifice — putting your hand in the wolf's mouth deals psychic damage but does not actually take the hand; (7) a sarcophagus at the center, trapped to collapse the entire barrow if opened. 2026-09-22-shearing-the-wolf

Npc 2

The White Lady (Queen of the Lake) Moon-tied spirit or goddess contacted through cold seance-work at the White Lady Inn. When she answers, frost traces itself on the windows and candles gutter with plumes of smoke. Bristled at Roadie's small-talk 'how you doing?' and retaliated by freezing Rico's exposed cock into a camel toe. Speaks in terse fragments — 'east coast, north.' Considers the gold in her lake her own.

Paxton A dwarven prosthetic-maker known in the Icewind Dale / Dwarven Valley area, familiar to Kaldur. His signature: fully articulated mechanical hands with tools extruding from the fingers. Normally works on the right hand. The one-armed Fang Finn Oaf wears a left-handed Paxton and confirms he knows Paxton personally. 2026-09-22-shearing-the-wolf

Faction 3

Church of Inexorable Ruin (Cult of Ragnarok) A doomsday cult also known as the Cult of Ragnarok. Members wear white robes and spiral-marked cudgels, with a spiral tattoo on the forehead. Serve Thrynne the Black. Doctrine: 'Harmony. The stillness of nothing.' Goal: acquire Uror's Shears, shear the Chaos Wolf, wake it, and let it devour the sun.

Raided East Haven's Town Hall for the shears; the party wiped the initial cell (five dead, two slept, one captured and interrogated). A larger contingent (16 by Kaldur's tremorsense) is now closing in on the party inside the William Moon barrow.

Fangs of Vanagander A mercenary company opposing the Cult of Ragnarok — specifically opposed to 'interpreting chaos.' One of their number was murdered for information about Uror's Shears. Another, Finn Oaf — the one-armed thief with a Paxton left-hand prosthetic and a Cape of the Mont Blanc — stole the shears from Speaker Daneth to keep them out of Thrynne's hands and holed up in the William Moon barrow. 'Scar' is another Fang, currently unaccounted for. 2026-09-22-shearing-the-wolf
Red Dragon Trading Company Luskan-based trading company 'with no scruples,' willing to move goods sourced from grave robbers. Finn Oaf named them as the intermediary that sold the shears — originally stolen from a burial mound — to Speaker Daneth as 'Urogears.' 2026-09-22-shearing-the-wolf

Item 4

Uror's Shears The Norn shears of Urðr — said to cut through anything, then return to the Norns after a single use. The Cult of Ragnarok wants them to shear the Chaos Wolf. Finn Oaf stole them from Speaker Daneth's office the morning of the raid and hid them in the William Moon barrow. 2026-09-22-shearing-the-wolf
Shardalyn A dark crystal infused from Crenshinabon during its last awakening ~30-40 years ago, mined by the Duergar in the Argonaut era. Whispers to those near it, drives listeners toward corruption. Speaker Daneth keeps a shardalyn statue in his office; it told the party 'the awakened one lies below.' 2026-09-22-shearing-the-wolf
Gorilla Panic A Mystican unregulated hallucinogen sold in packet form. Made in Mystica ('not pharmaceutically regulated over there'). Causes vivid hostile hallucinations — spider-gorillas, ripping off one's own clothes, running naked into snow. Roadie keeps multiple doses and a tea variant that hits within seconds; both packet and tea have been deployed on party and ambassador alike this session. 2026-09-22-shearing-the-wolf
Rocket-powered geyser sleds Ice sleds propelled by a decanter of endless water strapped to the back — 'basically a jet ski, but instead of water you fall on jagged ice.' Steer by angling the geyser. Leaves a trail of sheet ice behind. About 40 feet per round faster than an axe-beak mount. Exorbitantly expensive and impractical — 'only a madman would have built these.' The cult uses them; the party now has four. 2026-09-22-shearing-the-wolf
