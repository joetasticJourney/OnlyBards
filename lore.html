The Norns and Ginnungagap Norse-styled cosmology intersecting with Rico Lunae's Selûnian moon-god (William Moon = Odin). Ginnungagap is the yawning void of space between crystal spheres — a rigidly-gravitied plane in the William Moon barrow's entrance chamber. The three Norns are Urðr (age, looking back, wears white), Verðandi (present, wears earth tones and metal colors), and Skuld (hidden future, wears blues). Their shears cut fate itself and return to them after each use. 2026-09-22-shearing-the-wolf
Aurel A cold entity who used to receive sacrifices in the old ways — including through a ritual pattern the Cold-handed Man appears to be reviving. Aurel is dead: per Big Fat Knucklehead hearsay, killed years back by 'a gnome with a giant cock,' incinerated at the Black Cabin, and — per Kaldur's uncle's version, in which Kaldur's uncle saved him — reborn in Phoenix fire. Also lends his name to Aurel's Tears, a deep-blue drop of liquor served at the Wet Trout.
