Session 10: Alec Loves Alex Jones Read transcript →

The Moonmobile's expedition into Tourmaline's gem mine opens still staring down the lost dwarven mining party — gnomic ceremorphs flanking a muscle-bound 'mesomorph' mind flayer with a two-handed blade and a scar across one eye. Before Rico can regret face-chugging the bag of kobold red crystals, a ceremorph levels a conical glass stair-stepped laser pistol — and the fight becomes a scramble for who gets one first.

Roadie grapples the gunner and dangles him on a rope over the pit, pointedly refusing to kill him. Scales teleports in with Kinny Jay's glove and the brass bell, takes a halberd, and marks the big one as hunted prey. The mesomorph counter-attacks by driving tentacles into every orifice Scales has — which Scales' greased-up anus repeatedly rebuffs — while Dreadie's Heat Metal forces the Mind Blade out of his hands. Kaldur, watching the little ceremorph dominate Roadie into jet-black eyes, reads the glow and realizes there's no elder brain here. This mesomorph is a replacement hive-mind conduit, and he's doing it badly.

Then Dreadie decides to acrobat across the chasm. DC 16. He fails by six. Thirty-one points of fall damage leave him at five HP at the bottom of the pit — no teeth, eyes swollen shut, legs 'like some sort of pasta dish.' William Moon appears next to him with a sprawling contract Dreadie can't read; a bloody thumbprint takes care of consent, and his legs reknit. The fine print: declare publicly that William Moon saved you, full-back tattoo within six months, carte blanche retaliation clause buried in the subtext. 'By the power of William,' Dreadie deadpans up at the fight, 'I smite you' — and vicious-mockeries a ceremorph into paste. 'That's convert number four for Rico.'

Kaldur caps combat by grabbing one of the dropped laser pistols — autistic-artificer-who-just-saw-the-internet gaping — and squeezing a shot that turns a ceremorph's head into a canoe. He eyes the gun like Vlad the Impaler meeting Marshmallow memes. Scales imbues his spear with necrotic energy ('abominations must perish') and nearly drops Tharsarelli; Enjee finishes the mesomorph with a thunder blast 20 points over, splattering him into 'a pretty picture.' The band loots his psychic-damage greatsword, three laser pistols, a six-dwarf claim's worth of amethysts (~6000 gp from the surface alone), and a doer-purple spell gem for Scales.

A trap-door staircase reveals Tharsarelli's workshop and his biography. Letters confirm he was the last survivor of four ceremorph handmaidens to the elder brain Zilix-4, who once tried to bind himself to a kraken's brain and was thwarted at the Ruins of a Scar in the Sea of Swords by a hero named Theagenes and a poop-addicted doctor called Tinker Driven. Kaldur walks away with a shelf of magic-item schematics (amulet of health; masterwork trident). Enjee pockets a Cloak of the Manta Ray. Scales finds a far-realm genetic modification chamber and does what Scales does: shoves Thwip in with a loose brain labeled Abby Normal, a spider egg, a giant eye-covered monster hand, and a crystal ball. Enjee, who can't read a word of any of this, cheerfully flips the switch on a natural 20. Thwip emerges fused with the spider, abdomen-webs included. Spider-Thwip.

Scales caps the lab tour by cracking a nearby jar of gas, which turns out to be a bound invisible stalker. The stalker beats Scales unconscious against the wall, then calmly flies back to its jar and shuts the lid itself. Roadie seriously considers tying the jar to his back for later deployment; the jar bubbles threateningly and he reconsiders. He straps the giant eye-covered monster hand to his pack instead — 'for when someone loses a hand.'

At the Blue Clam, Spider-Thwip skanks on the ceiling like a cockroach-diapered Spider-Man, falls, knocks himself out, and web-shits onto Scales mid-set — which Scales dutifully mixes with his torso oil as grease-styling for the final solo. Dreadie's first performance under contract manifests as angelic choir overtones behind his harmonies. Rico almost fluffs the number watching Scales' greasy man-titties but remembers his training. The bar pays 30 gp each, and a Luskin music scout slips Scales his information: cylinder-label connections, if they want them. The Chuggy Pig rolls in. Everyone hits level five.

During the long rest, Rico reads deeper into the Book of Munnen and finds the Gospel of Answer — a warning about four false friends who try to keep Answer Moonsong in the Shadowfell before he fades: Lagic, Luella, Radivast, Mathis. The one true friend, Trellian, gave Answer a crystal. The book's transcriber is Answer Moonsong himself — meaning Rico is Zildor Moonsault's grandson, chosen-of-chosens. Rico asks William Moon for psionic powers to unlock the mind blade; William tells him to dip the sword in sacred waters at Moonsong Hill and chant 'Willy Moon Moon.' Meanwhile Dreadie pores over his contract at the bar with the hardest stare a gay man can produce, and Kaldur — the only other person who can see the text — runs a religion check. The contract reads celestial, closer to infernal than fae. No angel would write this. Kaldur's stomach bottoms out as he asks the question nobody has asked aloud yet: is William Moon a god? William spits out a cup of bad espresso behind him and makes another.
