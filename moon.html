Overview

Rico Lunae's cradle religion, carried into the band through his lead-guitarist's zealotry and now exported, one awkward conversation at a time, to anyone the band meets. Rico's own line: 'Have you heard the good news about William Moon?' The faith venerates Selûne as the one true god and William Moon as her chosen moon-knight / messenger — Rico insists he is 'chosen by Selûne to represent her.' Rico calls initiates 'Moonanites' and tried to brand the band 'Men of Moonlight' before Chaos Wolf (spelled Q-H-A-O-S V-U-L-F) stuck as the public name. As of session 3, William Moon canonized the band-wide activation phrase 'By the power of William Moon, I possess the wherewithal!' — raise a weapon, shout it, level up on the spot. Rico and Dreadie have both cashed it; Scales, Kaldur, Enjee, and Roadie have all firmly refused.

Cosmology

Rico has gaslit the party, with DM-rewarded success, into believing a specific syncretic cosmology: Odin was William Moon in disguise. The plane of Isgard contains both Asgard (the Norse realm) and Argenteel (Selûne's domain) as sibling realms. Selûne is the one true god; William Moon is her moon-knight / chosen representative, operating out of the World Tree. Kostov the Ratatouille moon-knight claims to be from the World Tree and to have 'walked its bowels,' and is openly a fellow disciple of William Moon — introduced in session 3. 'The moon has three phases' is a repeated William Moon catechism (contested by Kaldur, who notes correctly that it has four). The Norns and Ginnungagap are also canon in this cosmology — Uror's Shears belong to Urðr, and the William Moon barrow preserves the giant-slayer story of the three demons who cut up Orgelmir to build the world.

William Moon

Appears as a man-shaped figure with a shadow that casts horns — Dreadie's running theory is that he is Loki. Rewrites his own tenets in real time (session 3: Rico asked him to clarify Tenet #8, 'don't be gay,' and William Moon added an asterisk clarifying that 'gay stuff is fine, actually'). Freezes time to deliver personal cutscenes (session 3: elevated Rico to 'my moon knight, my paladin of lunanescent suffocation'). Appears to Dreadie in multiple forms — a fisherman by a docked ship, a big-titted woman flashing the Wet Trout crowd, and in-person for private arguments. Has personally confirmed: 'I did once bang a horse' (session 3); 'I promise I'll fix your teeth' to Dreadie; and 'the weave of fate has decided that your band is going to be at the crux of survival or destruction.' In session 4 he teleport-abducted Dreadie out of a Wet Trout outhouse into a private room to proclaim Rico and Dreadie 'my two disciples'; Dreadie refused and jumped out the window. In Rico's blackout-drug dream at the same inn, he reaffirmed Rico as 'the moon sign' (because the name 'Rico' means 'the chosen one'), invented the Tenet of Prioritization on the spot, and warned Rico that 'moon elves have a lacking enzyme that helps them break down gorilla panic.' Dreadie's working diagnosis after all of it: 'Pretty sure he's Loki.'

The Tablets

The religion's scripture is called the Tablets of William Moon — Rico has never actually seen the original tablets. What he carries is his dad's translation, printed as a pamphlet called the Book of Moon-nites. The pamphlet has at least 41 numbered tenets plus unnumbered maxims; Rico hands them out as evangelism aids and sometimes consults them mid-conversation to argue a point. Enjee involuntarily memorized the entire 41+ tenets in session 3 by sleeping upright next to Rico in the 'cuck chair' for four hours while Rico read the whole thing aloud to what he believed was a sleeping Enjee — Enjee's purple gelatinous eyes don't close, so Rico never knew. The pamphlet rewrites itself in real time when William Moon personally edits a tenet (session 3: Rico watched new asterisked fine-print appear mid-conversation about Tenet #8).

The Flock

Among the band: Rico Lunae is fully converted and now operates as William Moon's paladin of lunanescent suffocation, with the party-wide 'By the power of William Moon' level-up ladder as his new recruitment tool. Dreadie Mercurie took the bait once (leveled up during the barrow fight) and has since treated the whole William Moon phenomenon as an infiltration op — refuses discipleship, believes he is Loki, has survived two personal William Moon audiences (session 3 and session 4) by cutting both short and jumping out a window the second time. Scales, Kaldur, Enjee, and Roadie have all firmly refused the ladder — Kaldur specifically swore 'by the power of the Borodin Sarmine, I'm not fucking saying that' and chose to stay a level behind rather than take the bribe. Outside the band: Kostov the Ratatouille, encountered mid-barrow fight in session 3, claims to be a level-4 moon knight from the World Tree and openly a disciple — though Kaldur's insight check caught him pausing strangely every time he named William Moon, so his exact relationship to the faith is still an open question. Rico's conversion pitch has survived into session 4 (he tried, and failed, to convert Dzan the Red Wizard in his cell on the morning of the burning gig).
