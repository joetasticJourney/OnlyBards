Session Notes 6

Session 11: The Moose was Loose A banner Moon session with deepening cosmology red flags. Rico Lunae personally baptizes Yorlt the Goliath dancer in the Lucky Liar parking lot — 'by the power of the moon I possess the wherewithal' — summoning William Moon on cue to ratify the conversion. William earnestly fields Yorlt's question about what it feels like when the moonlight 'comes inside' followers ('I'm feeling every inch of it'), reveals he intends to come inside 'the whole world' before it's over, finger-guns a literal 'poof' exit, and formally refers Yorlt to Anzor's Moon Straight School — ASS — now canon as the official Latter-Day Selunite gay conversion academy. Rico teaches Yorlt the dance-away-the-gay exercise (push the thoughts way down, snuff them out like a torch). William then slides into Dreadie Mercurie's table at The Lucky Liar drinking from a silver goblet shaped as a serpent coiled around the cup, full of honey mead 'my brother made.' Dreadie quietly logs the mismatch: serpent iconography, an invoked brother, and honey mead do not read as Selûne-adjacent. William commissions a Dreadie song about himself but requires anonymization in the lyrics — he 'operates in the shadows'; Dreadie must refer to him in the chorus as 'Flash Gordon.' Dreadie proposes serpent imagery for the song; William smoothly deflects and tells him not to worry about the imagery. Dreadie drafts the working title 'The Trick of the Moonlight' on the spot. Before the exit, a new contract clause binds Dreadie to address William as 'Big Daddy' going forward. Dreadie checks the pages, complies — 'Later, Big Daddy!' — then cheers at the empty chair: 'Dreadie tolerates you!' Dreadie admits to William, directly and aloud, that he has been feeling 'horrible, baleful' in William's service; William's response is a friendly 'love you.' The religion's angel-of-Selûne cover is wearing visibly thin. Smaller notes: William reminds Rico mid-conversation that his (Dreadie's) legs are 'feeling not like pasta' now — a reference to Session 10's contract healing. Rico claims Yorlt as 'convert number nine' (post-session tally since the pamphlet distribution started). The 'always skip leg day' quip that closes the session is a Kaldur joke, not actual doctrine, and should not be adopted as a tenet.
Session 10: Alec Loves Alex Jones The biggest Moon session yet. William Moon personally appears at the bottom of an 87-foot pit next to a dying Dreadie Mercurie, offers a sprawling contract Dreadie cannot read, and accepts a bloody thumbprint in lieu of signature. The deal regrows Dreadie's legs and binds him to publicly credit William with the save, get a full-back tattoo within six months, and tolerate a carte-blanche retaliation clause. Dreadie's first contract-mandated performance at the Blue Clam gig manifests as literal angelic-choir harmonies — and he immediately Vicious-Mockery-kills a ceremorph with 'By the power of William, I smite you.' Rico calls it convert number four. William also appears behind Rico and Scales as they climb out of the mine, doing the robot. In Rico's room during the long rest, he drops into a recliner and reveals the path to unlocking the mesomorph greatsword's extra psychic d6: Moonsong Hill pilgrimage, dip the sword in sacred waters, chant 'Willy Moon Moon.' Hints that psionic power requires 'centuries in the far realm or genetic experimentation in a species's past' and tells Rico to 'read your contract' — teasing deeper lineage lore. Rico in turn reads Kaldur the Gospel of Answer from the Book of Munnen for the first time: Answer Moonsong (Rico's grandfather Zildor Moonsault) was stranded in the Shadowfell and warned of four false friends (Lagic, Luella, Radivast, Mathis) and one true friend (Trellian, who gave him a crystal) before fading home. The religion's own scripture is now a family document, written by Rico's grandfather. Kaldur, investigating Dreadie's copied contract via religion check, identifies it as celestial-leaning-infernal — explicitly not fae — and asks the party-level question nobody has asked aloud: is William Moon a god, not an angel? William spits out a cup of bad espresso behind him and makes another. Scales also starts probing Rico about Moon-paladin oaths (and offers himself as 'a hand to hold'), suggesting a potential upcoming theological crossover between Oath of the Eldritch Hunt and the Latter-Day Selunites.
Session 9: Tip Toes: Role of a Lifetime William Moon appeared twice on-camera this session. First, jogging on his back alongside the Moonmobile after Kaldur punched Rico in the mouth for the dwarf-face scheme, kissing his finger to Rico's split lip to heal it and whispering that Rico is his 'special play' who has 'never done anything wrong' — a direct cosmological co-sign of the Mooney Moonbeard scheme over Kaldur's objection. Second, materializing a crescent-moon helmet and water wings for the resurrected kobold Thwip ('Shablagoon'), plus a crescent-moon-stamped diaper bag for Dreadie (who used it to change Thwip's diaper). Rico added three tenets to the Book of Munnen this session (psionics are unclean; crescent-moon scars are a blessing; short face in service of Saloon), and personally handed a copy of the book to Lord Stokely Silverstream after the Dwarven Valley gig. Rico also revealed in-play, for the first time to Kaldur, that his father was born of divine moonlight at Moonsong Hill, which Rico takes as proof he is the Chosen One; Kaldur has started privately granting that the Moonsong Hill cosmology may be real.
Session 8: Chardalyn Gone Wild William Moon made two live appearances this session. He mimed strangling himself with his cane at Dreadie through the Northern Light window to shut up Roadie and Enjee's William-Moon ribbing. Later, at the Terror gig, he physically entered the stage to touch his finger-cane to Rico's forehead and vacuum the Rico-mutation eyeballs away, visibly in horror, declaring Tenet 89 — 'Two eyes only' — as a stop-gap doctrinal fix. Rico interpreted the Moon's horror-and-save as additional blessing, and added Tenets 27 and 40 of his own — Dreadie and Roadie did not contest the numbering, so they are treated as canon by the band going forward.
Session 7: Jail House Rock Three new theological developments this session. First, Rico's lineage: Oracle Ethel Arkoron directed him to Moonsong Hill to learn the truth of his bloodline, confirming (via Rico's own statement) that his family is 'the Honorable Moonsong Clan' and that his father was conceived there. Second, Moonsong Hill is a major religious site for the actual Selünite people, not the Latter-Day Selünites — Rico has conflated the two. Third, William Moon himself admitted this session he does not know where Moonsong Hill is, and reiterated his omniscience is bounded by 'whatever the moonlight touches.' William Moon also manifested at Rico's bedside at the Northern Light while Rico was oiling up Yorlt, awarded him 'Double Moon Deluxe Madness,' and quizzed him on the double-secret hidden tenets (which Rico has not actually read and bluffed through). The pamphlet for the Latter-Day Saloon Nights is now distributed in handful quantities: Rico handed brochures to Virgats the Wretched (who rolled his eyes), Trovis (slipped into his pocket with two silver pieces for luck), Warden Pepperfist's noble peerage, and the Revel's End crowd via thrown Book of Munin copies during the jailhouse gig.
Session 6: O'Cosby's Revenge Session 6 is the religion's single biggest doctrinal and visible-miracle session of the campaign. Four things moved. First, Rico performed the first unambiguous verifiable miracle: he laid the Book of Munin on Tam Sharf's throat (she'd been mute for years from a half-successful bungee-cord hanging) and William Moon's regenerative light closed the scar and gave her back her voice in front of the whole party. Second, Tam Sharf — speaking for the first time in years — immediately asked Rico how much William Moon forgives, hypothetically — how bad of a thing. Rico's throwaway reply ('is it a gay thing? No? Just murder? You'll be okay then.') now stands as the religion's informal position on murder. Tam then took the Book of Munin out of Rico's hands and began reading it: 'Oh, Silvery Moon, huh?' She is the first non-Rico reader on the record. Third, William Moon manifested in the basement cell and spoke THROUGH Kaldur Gravelgut's own throat to brief Rico, who missed it entirely; Dreadie heard it; Kaldur experienced it only as 'something's pissing me off, I can't put my finger on it.' Kaldur did not grab or read the book, but is now a converted skeptic. Fourth, Rico casually confirmed two new facts about Latter-Day Selunite culture that are now canon: Rico has four moms (polygamy applies in the religion), and the religion is also sometimes called 'Latter-Day Saloon Nights.' Two new numbered tenets (32 and 88) were established in-session.
