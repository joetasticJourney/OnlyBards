Session Notes 2

Session 7: Jail House Rock Three new theological developments this session. First, Rico's lineage: Oracle Ethel Arkoron directed him to Moonsong Hill to learn the truth of his bloodline, confirming (via Rico's own statement) that his family is 'the Honorable Moonsong Clan' and that his father was conceived there. Second, Moonsong Hill is a major religious site for the actual Selünite people, not the Latter-Day Selünites — Rico has conflated the two. Third, William Moon himself admitted this session he does not know where Moonsong Hill is, and reiterated his omniscience is bounded by 'whatever the moonlight touches.' William Moon also manifested at Rico's bedside at the Northern Light while Rico was oiling up Yorlt, awarded him 'Double Moon Deluxe Madness,' and quizzed him on the double-secret hidden tenets (which Rico has not actually read and bluffed through). The pamphlet for the Latter-Day Saloon Nights is now distributed in handful quantities: Rico handed brochures to Virgats the Wretched (who rolled his eyes), Trovis (slipped into his pocket with two silver pieces for luck), Warden Pepperfist's noble peerage, and the Revel's End crowd via thrown Book of Munin copies during the jailhouse gig.
Session 6: O'Cosby's Revenge Session 6 is the religion's single biggest doctrinal and visible-miracle session of the campaign. Four things moved. First, Rico performed the first unambiguous verifiable miracle: he laid the Book of Munin on Tam Sharf's throat (she'd been mute for years from a half-successful bungee-cord hanging) and William Moon's regenerative light closed the scar and gave her back her voice in front of the whole party. Second, Tam Sharf — speaking for the first time in years — immediately asked Rico how much William Moon forgives, hypothetically — how bad of a thing. Rico's throwaway reply ('is it a gay thing? No? Just murder? You'll be okay then.') now stands as the religion's informal position on murder. Tam then took the Book of Munin out of Rico's hands and began reading it: 'Oh, Silvery Moon, huh?' She is the first non-Rico reader on the record. Third, William Moon manifested in the basement cell and spoke THROUGH Kaldur Gravelgut's own throat to brief Rico, who missed it entirely; Dreadie heard it; Kaldur experienced it only as 'something's pissing me off, I can't put my finger on it.' Kaldur did not grab or read the book, but is now a converted skeptic. Fourth, Rico casually confirmed two new facts about Latter-Day Selunite culture that are now canon: Rico has four moms (polygamy applies in the religion), and the religion is also sometimes called 'Latter-Day Saloon Nights.' Two new numbered tenets (32 and 88) were established in-session.
