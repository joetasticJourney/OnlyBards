Session 8: Chardalyn Gone Wild Read transcript →

Scales spent the whole session off-stage, chemically dead in a Chuggy Pig bedroom. The player had been up until 4 a.m. the night before, so Kaldur's canon was that Scales was 'doing heroin in his room — the kind distilled from poppy, that you put on a spoon and heat up and inject into your veins.' The band went to work short one brass dragonborn.

At the Northern Light, prep for the evening show spiraled immediately. Roadie offered Rico a pinch of mystery drug pocket — Rico, mystery-powder-pilled, drew Blinker Powder, teleported thirty feet outside, then teleported back in face-first into Ali Sherrard's chest (Roadie later revealed the gorilla panic packet Rico had refused was the harmless green-tea decoy). While Rico was rebooting, Enjee stripped out of his linen pants to oil Yorlt's calves, and his own confusingly enormous flaccid penis dragged on the floor. Kaldur, across the room, spotted a tiny star-shaped birthmark on the left side of Enjee's dickhead — identical to the famously enormous, floating, floor-dragging genitalia of one Osifred 'Ozzy' Grimmund, court wizard of King Havel. The dwarf went quietly nuclear.

The gig was a legendary critical success. Kaldur slap-bassed; Dreadie counted it in; Rico gave Yorlt a dedicated bardic inspiration and then jumped off the table, teleported through the ceiling, landed in the snow on the roof, and teleported back inside landing face-first on Yorlt's thigh. Yorlt helicopter-dicked a single drop of dew into a lady's beer. Enjee solo'd. The crowd lost its mind, and the band walked out with Yorlt and both Sherrard sisters as full-time 'Solid Gold Dancers.' Mir and Alisar skimmed the bar tips like Kaldur taught them.

In the post-show hush, Kaldur leaned on Enjee: you knew Ozzy. Yes — technically my father, kind of. Ozzy made him, same way he made Ratabast, out of 'his own skeleton he somehow got out of his body' — Enjee is an ooze over a dwarf skeleton, a purple T-1000 with a vestigial anus that eats peanuts for flavor. He grew up second fiddle to Ratabast. Enjee's tenure as the band's drummer is partly a Witness Protection arrangement.

From there, back to the Terror — the Shardalyn dragon corpse hollowed out and squatting in Kerr Konig. Virgats the Wretched, an Ilmatarian priest, demanded to be chained to a statue through the links by his own balls (he squeezed his nuts through the iron rings, moaning), then absorbed the dragon's psychic corruption into himself with gusto. Three Duergar specters and an invisible poltergeist — the last tenants of this dragon, who died when something came up from the Underdark — tried to kill the band before Virgats could finish. Dreadie's spirit guide this fight was a headless ghostly Kurt Cobain who shot the sharpshooter beat.

Inside the cleansed dragon, the party looted an inn that had been crushed and repurposed. Kaldur found the actual paving stone King Havel killed the Shadow Dragon with — and, nearly weeping at the vindication, decided it was a holy site and left it exactly where it lay. They found a shield with the mark of Deep Duerra, a double-visored magic helm, a ghostly dwarven battle-axe haft, a brewery with a Gibbering Mouther trapped in a still, a bathing drow siren in a sub-floor pool who made out with Rico so hard underwater he nearly drowned, and, in the sub-cellar, a wine room with five special bottles plus the Queen of Moonlight's own Moon Mead. The drow transferred Rico a very friendly STD called Kraken's Kiss — a tiny tentacle that now emerges from his urethra and can hold a guitar pick. By session's end, Kaldur was sketching a double-neck cock-guitar strap design on the back of the Duergar ledger.

Kaldur, using a +3 Magecraft'd brewing kit, distilled the Gibbering Mouther into a single jug of Gibbering Moonshine. Roadie pocketed it. They refused to give Rico any. The party found the cellar chasm that leads straight to the Underdark — same chasm the Duergar who built this place came up through — and left it unsealed, for later.

Second gig: back at the Terror, charging one silver a beer, with the dragon corpse itself tapped as a psionic amplifier via Roadie's bass cable (Kaldur jury-rigged it). The band smashed it — a 25, a 25, a natural 20, a bumpable one-tick-from-green. Then, caught up in the moment, Rico demanded a shot of the Gibbering Moonshine mid-set. He failed the DC-17 Con save, and eyeballs opened all over his body — palms, back of hands, forearms, face. The crowd screamed and ran. William Moon manifested and poked a finger-cane to Rico's forehead to put the eyeballs away, declaring (in horror) a brand-new tenet: 'Two eyes only.' Roadie covered it as a silent-image special-effect gaff and talked the crowd into a free-round encore. Critical success, +3 renown. The Terror is now the band's official bastion, with an open hallway to the Underdark in the basement.
