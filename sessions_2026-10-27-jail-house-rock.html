Session 7: Jail House Rock Read transcript →

The band wakes up still inside the Caer with one loose thread: the one-legged dwarven oracle upstairs. Her name is Ethel Arkoron — blind, lame, Levistus priestess, former rocker ('I gave a double handy at Woodstock 99'). Because Levistus's beef with chaos aligns with the party, she offers one answer each. Kaldur gets a Delzunian crypt behind the Shining Falls. Scales learns he carries two divine bloodlines. Rico is sent to Moonsong Hill. Dreadie gets Virgats the Wretched's name. Roadie whispers for Bonothin Decavis's phylactery and gets the Blood Swamp in Grotzt. Enjee is told it was the green one, not the white one, who sent him. Ethel finishes her last answer, says a flat 'ah,' and dies on the spot. Enjee loots a packet of powdered goat milk from her pocket.

On the way out, the band finds a bedazzled rocket sled in the street, tagged 'Rico and Dreadie' and labeled 'the Moonmobile.' Command word: by the power of the moon. It ejaculates glowing moon-juice out of a rear sphincter, leaves a glitter wake, and handles like a dream — so long as the command word gets spoken once a minute. Dreadie is horrified his name is on it. Kaldur begins carving Delzunian runes into the chassis in protest.

At Kaer Konig the band finds the Shardalyn-corrupted dragon corpse at Moonsong Hill — 'a place where spirits yearn to rock,' per Thrynne the Black's note. Roadie wants to install amps inside the corpse and rock it awake. Kaldur religion-checks and says any priest trying to purify it unaided would stroke out — they need Virgats. Rico cites Tenet 72 ('Don't fuck with Shardalyn') as both scripture and paladin vow. Roadie reluctantly agrees to go get the priest first.

At the Northern Light the band meets the current speaker Trovis, a vomit-soaked raging-alcoholic sociopath. He insults the barmaid Allie Sherrard; her Goliath brother-in-law Yorlt drops his satchel and rolls initiative. Dreadie Suggests Trovis 'take the night off and leave your daughters undisturbed' — the DM lets it land despite Trovis having no daughters, and Trovis trudges upstairs in confused obedience. Pinia Galvain, a halfling fan from the East Haven gig, clonks him with a staff. The paddy wagon arrives Rocko's-Modern-Life-style. Pinia announces she's running against him in two weeks.

Roadie books the Sherrard sisters as dancers for the next night: 20 gp each, plus 1 gp extra so Allie's bodice stays on. Rico adds Yorlt to the lineup as a greased muscle-dancer at twenty-five gold. Scales is milked for lubricant and gives it willingly ('I don't look a gift horse in the math'). Rico and Kaldur then spend the evening oiling Yorlt down 'with mechanical precision' while the winter scouts at the next table push their chowder away in horror. In Rico's room that night William Moon manifests, awards Rico 'Double Moon Deluxe Madness,' quizzes him on the double-secret hidden tenets, and admits — when pressed — that he does not know where Moonsong Hill actually is.

At dawn the band blasts up to Revel's End in the Moonmobile. Roadie glimpses a red-glowing tree at the summit of Kelvin's Cairn in passing. Virgats the Wretched is a scarred, prosthetic-armed, Werner-Herzog-accented Ilmatarian priest running the Revel's End infirmary ('I have to sew up all the assholes'). Warden Pepperfist greets Kaldur as a fellow wax-cylinder recording nerd and books the band on the spot: 200 gp flat plus 50% of wax-cylinder sales, the band's biggest-ever payday.

The jailhouse concert starts in the red — 312 confused prisoners don't recognize Chaos Wolf. Rico brings it to orange. Roadie fashions a codpiece out of an endless-water decanter and spray-geysers the crowd like Danzig. Dreadie counts it up to yellow. Scales anchors with a dirty 25. Enjee lands a Neil Peart drum solo on a natural 20. The meter cracks green. Then Roadie, under direct instructions from his undead bat Vrock, grabs a handful of whatever is in his drug pocket and spraygeyser-blasts it into the audience — rolling a 76 on the drug table (infernal/necromantic/forbidden) and 144 on the demonic mutation table. One prisoner, Gregory, sprouts huge wings, grabs a guard, and flies off to be eaten by demons. Roadie and Dreadie play it off as a stage effect with a 25 performance/deception combo. Warden Pepperfist buys it: 'I can't believe they got Gregory to be in on that — he hates performance.' Twenty platinum change hands.

On the ride back Vrock vomits Gregory's ring finger into Roadie's palm as confirmation. Roadie quietly adds it to his growing pouch of Vrock-donated fingers. At Kaer Konig, Virgats plants his cudgel at the base of the Shardalyn dragon and begins to chant.
