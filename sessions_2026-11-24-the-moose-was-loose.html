Session 11: The Moose was Loose Read transcript →

Half the band is out before the session starts. Scales is in the Chuggy Pig doing heroin 'the whole day and night' and Enjee never shows. The three bards who remain re-sync their sheets and the Moonmobile strap-locks to the Chuggy Pig for the run to Lonelywood.

Ten miles out, something flattened on the road: a gored male in fur-lined winter gear, right shin crushed, entrails out. Rico eyes the hoof prints and calls it — a moose, specifically one with antlers, specifically possessed by Shar. Roadie sprinkles crack on the body and tries Spare the Dying on it (rolls a 3), then rolls the corpse up on his pizza peel 'as is tradition' and tucks it under the carriage. Also spotted along the southern coastline: a glowing-purple Chardalyn Tree installation, fenced off like the first zone of an MMO expansion.

In Lonelywood, Nipsey Huddle (the town speaker) greets the band at The Lucky Liar and Danae Zotal — a deadpan goth bartender — negotiates them down to 30 gp. Danae IDs the dead man as one of Nirth Max Zildanar's pilferers out of Targos. Harry Jack and the boys — werewolf hunters who, per Kaldur's Arcana, 'smell like dog urine and are covered in wolf fur' and are themselves werewolves — are camped at the sketchy table pitching themselves for the ghost-moose bounty; Roadie gifts them a pitcher with a handful of ayahuasca mystery-powder in it, winks Brunhilde the Goliath dancer a backstage pass (she snaps a carrot clean in half with her vag, catapulting half of it into his chin — 'Dio works in mysterious ways'), and Rico oils up Yorlt behind the bar until his huge dick drips off.

The gig starts with the entire tripping crowd formed into a Zion-rave pile and Dreadie flouncing Freddie-style around the mic stand under soft purple and pink lights. Kaldur freezes mid-bass line when Danae makes extended direct eye contact ('oh god she's still looking at me, oh god she's still looking at me'); Rico leaps across the stage, bounces off him like a granite statue, and finishes his guitar solo on his knees at Kaldur's girdle with grease-drip spattering. The crowd exalts — some spontaneously sprouting dreadlocks — and the gig lands clean. 30 gp each plus 2 gp in ayahuasca-victim tips.

Out in the parking lot, Rico baptizes Yorlt as a Latter-Day Selunite — 'By the power of the moon I possess the wherewithal' — and William Moon pops in with the 'will-you-moon' robot hand-gesture to confirm the convert and personally interview the goliath. Yorlt asks what it feels like when William comes inside followers; William earnestly describes 'feeling every inch of it.' Dreadie, watching from around the corner, is physically restraining a laugh. Rico teaches Yorlt the dance-away-the-gay exercise ('push those thoughts way down') and recommends Anzor's Moon Straight School — ASS — a Moonanite conversion academy that he's, unprompted, now canon-named.

William Moon slides into Dreadie's table drinking from a silver goblet shaped as a serpent coiled all the way around the cup, poured full of mead 'my brother made.' Dreadie quirks a brow at that — the serpent iconography, the brother, the honey mead — none of it looks like Selûne's lane. William demands Dreadie write a song about him, but keep him anonymized — 'operate in the shadows, call me Flash Gordon in the chorus.' Dreadie drafts the working title 'The Trick of the Moonlight.' Before William poofs, the contract updates: Dreadie is now required to address William as 'Big Daddy.' Dreadie salutes the exit with a cheerful 'Later, Big Daddy!' and then cheers up at the empty table — 'Dreadie tolerates you!'

Kaldur, meanwhile, raw-dogs a four-hour bar conversation with Danae, deep in Gnomish red wine, where every single pass sails three feet over his head until she literally asks 'Would you like to hook up?' He does. Dreadie, watching from the tavern door, delivers an inward-turned thumbs-up as Kaldur stumbles back toward the Chuggy Pig with Danae's hair significantly disheveled. In the mead-warm afterglow, Danae gets drunk-sad on him — the bar emptying out around her — and nobody asks about the rug under the barstool with the suspiciously short leg, the one Kaldur's Nat-20 insight placed directly over Dzan's trap-door lair.

Two days from the full moon, the band heads out to finish the actual quest. The ghost moose manifests in the moonlight, materializes corporeal on d6 of 4-5-6 each round, and tramples Rico into the dirt before he can finish shouting 'William Moon!' Kaldur, who shaped his new ghost axe from the hunt for exactly this, attunes the handle to the moose's spectral vibration until his hands buzz at the same frequency as the ghost itself — 'like hitting a fucking tree stump' — and fells it clean. Roadie rides the help-action loop every round while Vrock, his familiar, flaps in for the flying attacks; Bo Vine, the cow-man security, stays with the Chuggy Pig in his old Zeke Gator band shirt. The speakers' pooled bounty (minus Dugan's Hole) comes in at 1000 gp, 250 each. On the ride out, Rico and Kaldur bicker about plate armor — 'everybody knows you don't need protection on the legs, tenet 60: always skip leg day' — and the Chuggy Pig turns south for Bremen.
